Inside the MCG

The Melbourne Cricket Ground (MCG) is a massive, awe-inspiring arena that holds over 100,000 people and delivers a roaring, electric atmosphere during major events. The Vibe and Stands Massive Scale: The grandstands tower high above the field, creating a colossal bowl of roaring fans. The Atmosphere: During a big AFL match or cricket test, the noise from the crowd echoes intensely across the turf. Sightlines: Most seats offer clear views of the action, though some upper tiers feel steep and high up. Behind the Scenes Player Sanctum: Walking through the player change rooms, the warm-up areas, and down the race onto the hollow turf feels historic and exclusive. The Long Room: This members-only area requires formal dress and exudes a classic, old-school cricket tradition. Australian Sports Museum: Located inside the stadium, it features deep sports history, memorabilia, and interactive exhibits.
